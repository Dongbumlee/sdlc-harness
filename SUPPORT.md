# Support

- **Questions and bug reports:** open a repository issue. Include
  the harness version, your client (VS Code or Copilot CLI), the
  phase you were in, and the relevant manifest or ledger excerpt —
  the run's records are the fastest route to a diagnosis.
- **MCP setup problems:** see
  [docs/mcp-troubleshooting.md](docs/mcp-troubleshooting.md) first.
- **Security issues:** do not use public issues — follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Understanding the workflow:** the walkthrough in
  [docs/workflow-guide.md](docs/workflow-guide.md) and the canonical
  definitions in [docs/base-definitions.md](docs/base-definitions.md)
  answer most process questions.
