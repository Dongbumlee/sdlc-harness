# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes everyone who shows up to build in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive — critique work, not people.
- Assume competence and good intent; disagreements about design
  are normal and are settled with evidence.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any project space.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block
participants who violate this code. Reports go to the maintainers
through GitHub (privately, via the repository's reporting tools)
and are handled in confidence.
